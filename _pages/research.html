My interests lie in the mathematical foundations of machine learning, with a particular focus on generalization theory and generative models.

My work on discrete diffusion models is supervised by Julie Delon, Rémi Gribonval, and Gabriel Peyré. Previously, I worked at Inria with the OCKHAM and GENESIS teams on PAC-Bayes bounds and symmetries in neural networks.

My background spans the MVA program and Mines Paris – PSL, with research experience at Harvard and applied work at Scortex.

  • Generalization & PAC-Bayes
  • Symmetries in neural networks
  • Discrete diffusion models
