## The question

What can a simple square tell us about an inequality?

<!--more-->

## A first example

For real numbers $a$ and $b$, a square is nonnegative:

$$
(a-b)^2 \geq 0.
$$

Expanding gives $a^2+b^2 \geq 2ab$, with equality exactly when $a=b$.
